## Overheard at Breakfast (TryHackMe, Hacker Holidays)
Category: OSINT | Difficulty: Easy | Room completed: 100%

## What is OSINT?
OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) means collecting information from
sources that are public and legal to access, such as social media,
websites and public records. No hacking is involved. The skill is
noticing small details and connecting them.

## What the room was about
A screenshot of a conversation between two people was given, and the
goal was to find a hidden online account using only the clues inside it.

## Method I used
1. **Read the whole thing slowly.** The room's own hint was to read what
   was said, not skim it.
2. **Extract clues.** I listed every detail that could identify
   someone: e.g. names, nicknames, places, interests, habits.
3. **Combine clues.** One detail alone is weak, but several together
   narrow it down.
4. **Search and verify.** I looked for the account on public platforms
   and checked that it matched the clues.
5. **Submit the result.**

## What I learned
- People leak information without noticing, in what they say, what
  is visible in photos, and what they name their accounts.
- Small details add up to a profile. This is why oversharing is a
  security risk.
- Hashing was one of the room's tags. A hash turns data into a fixed
  string of characters and cannot easily be reversed.
- Writing down clues in a list made the search much easier than
  keeping it all in my head.

## Why this matters for security
- Attackers use OSINT before an attack to learn about a target, for
  example to guess passwords or write convincing phishing messages
  (social engineering).
- Defenders and investigators use OSINT to check what an organisation
  exposes, and to trace threats.
- My own habits: avoid reusing usernames everywhere, don't post
  location or workplace details, and check what my public profiles show.

## Ethics and safety
- OSINT is only for lab rooms, my own accounts, or authorised work.
- In real life, finding private information about a person can lead to
  stalking or harassment, so I never do it to real people without
  permission.
- I do not post the flag or the answers from this room.
