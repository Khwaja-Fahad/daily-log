## What is cyber security?
Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and data
from attacks, theft, and damage. It covers keeping systems safe (defence)
and also understanding how attackers think (offence) so weaknesses can be
found and fixed before real criminals use them.

## 2 roles that sound interesting

### 1. SOC Analyst
Monitors alerts and logs, investigates suspicious activity, and helps
respond to incidents. Interesting because it's the front line of defence
and a common entry point into security.

### 2. Penetration Tester
Is authorised to attack systems in a safe, legal way to find weaknesses
before real attackers do, then writes reports explaining how to fix them.
Interesting because it's hands-on and creative problem solving.
